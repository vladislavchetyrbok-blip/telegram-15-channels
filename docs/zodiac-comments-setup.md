# Zodiac Comments Setup

This is an operator guide for comments/discussion strategy in the Telegram Zodiac network. It is documentation only: comments are configured manually in Telegram, not by the daily publisher.

## Recommended Strategy

1. Keep daily horoscope publishing independent from comments.
2. Use comments first only on the general zodiac channel.
3. Add sign-channel comments later, after each sign channel has a real audience and moderation capacity.
4. Use one dedicated Telegram discussion group for the general channel.
5. Keep the discussion group moderated and linked from the channel UI.
6. Do not let comments become a dependency for daily publishing.
7. Do not store discussion group IDs in source control unless they are public and intentionally documented.
8. Review moderation quality before enabling comments on all 12 sign channels.

## Phase 1: General Channel Only

Enable comments for the general zodiac channel first. This keeps discussion concentrated in one place and avoids 12 low-activity or unmoderated comment areas.

Suggested pinned discussion group text:

```text
Добро пожаловать в обсуждение гороскопов 🔮

Здесь можно делиться впечатлениями, задавать вопросы и обсуждать прогнозы.
Пожалуйста, общайтесь уважительно и без спама.
Навигация по всем знакам есть в закреплённом сообщении основного канала.
```

## Phase 2: Sign Channels

Enable comments per sign only when that sign channel has:

- stable daily posting;
- visible subscriber activity;
- a clear moderation routine;
- no spam or scam pressure in the general discussion.

## Risks

- Spam/scam comments can damage the premium feel of the network.
- Too many empty comment sections can make new channels look inactive.
- Linking the wrong discussion group can confuse subscribers.
- Comment setup is a Telegram channel setting, so it should be verified manually after every change.

## Operator Checklist

- General channel has the correct linked discussion group.
- Pinned navigation post remains visible.
- Comments are enabled only where intentionally approved.
- Moderators know how to remove spam and restrict users.
- Daily publishing, ledger, scheduler, and image assets are unchanged.
