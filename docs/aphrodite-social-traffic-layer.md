# Aphrodite Social Traffic Layer Architecture (Package 141)

## What this is

Package 141 creates a **read-only social traffic architecture** for Aphrodite across Instagram,
TikTok, Telegram, and YouTube Shorts. It defines the strategy, content pillars, viral hooks,
content templates, manual review flow, safe CTA rules, and safety boundaries.

It is **planning only** — no posting, no platform APIs, no scraping, no credentials.

## What this package explicitly does NOT do

- It does **not** implement auto-posting.
- It does **not** call Instagram / TikTok / YouTube / Telegram APIs.
- It does **not** store platform credentials.
- It does **not** scrape competitors.
- It does **not** copy competitor designs or texts (original Aphrodite voice only).
- It does **not** implement payments.
- It does **not** implement real VIP access.
- It does **not** modify cron, workflows, publish scripts, or bot-sending logic.
- It does **not** perform a production launch.

Daily and weekly automation remain **unblocked**. Manual Review remains UI / read-only.

## Product direction

Aphrodite social traffic sells **emotional outcomes**, not generic astrology. Hooks lead with the
questions users actually feel: does he love me, what does he feel, why does he pull away, what red
flags to notice, who is meant for me, what is coming next, what should I hear today. Each hook
offers a soft, original read and a **non-payment CTA** into the Telegram Mini App for a free
preview.

## Platforms

- Instagram — Reels, Stories, carousel / text cards.
- TikTok — short videos.
- Telegram — posts + Mini App entry.
- YouTube Shorts — future short-video layer.

## Content pillars

AI Love Reading, Soulmate Scanner, Red Flags Scanner, AI Future Timeline, Daily Message From
Universe, Zodiac compatibility, Angel numbers / mystic coincidences, Birth matrix hooks.

## Templates

Eight original safe templates (e.g. "If he pulls away…", "Your soulmate type by sign…", "One red
flag your sign ignores…", "What the next 30 days may bring…", "Message from the Universe today…",
"Angel number meaning…", "Birth matrix hidden pattern…", "Compatibility tension point…"). Each
template includes a hook, a 3-step structure, a safe Mini App CTA, and the blocked-claims list.

## Manual review flow

Draft → manual human review (claims, tone, blocked-claim check) → approved drafts exported for a
human to post manually. No step posts automatically.

## Safe CTAs and blocked claims

Safe CTAs (no payment): "Open Aphrodite in Telegram", "Get your free Love Reading preview",
"Check your relationship pattern", "Open your personal preview".
Blocked claims: guaranteed love, loyalty magic / spell, 100% prediction, "he will return",
deterministic prophecy, medical / legal / financial advice, manipulation tactics.
